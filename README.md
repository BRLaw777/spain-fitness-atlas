# Spain Top-10 Fitness Chains & Planet Fitness Interactive Atlas (November 2026)

Interactive geospatial atlas of 1,203 tracked Spanish fitness clubs (1,063 open + 140 pre-opening) across Spain's top-10 full-format chains plus Planet Fitness, overlaid onto INE 2025 census tracts (36,554 tracts) and INE ADRH 2024 tax-record demographics with 10-minute street-network drive and walk isochrones.
